About us

Arguing for India's upside

Upside India is an independent consumer organisation. We report good news about India's everyday economy, and argue for how India can reach its upside: growth, a lower cost of living and more freedom for consumers.

What we do

Three ways we get to the upside

Good news

We highlight what's working

When a rule change makes life cheaper or simpler somewhere in India, we explain it, show the numbers and ask who could do the same.

Smarter rules

We push for better rules

We show what taxes and rules add to everyday costs, and make the case for changes that cut them, with sources anyone can check.

Your voice

We take your voice to decision makers

Tell us which costs hurt most. We bring what you tell us to the officials, lawmakers and journalists who can change the rules.

How we work

The rules we follow

  1. 01

    Every number has a source

    It sits on the graphic or next to the figure, so you can check it yourself.

  2. 02

    On the consumer's side, not a party's

    We don't praise or attack any party or politician. We judge rules by what they do for the people who pay.

  3. 03

    Good news first

    We report what's working before we argue for what could be better.

  4. 04

    Instagram is our newsroom

    Stories, explainers and poll results go out on Instagram first. This site is where you find out who we are.

  5. 05

    We speak your language

FAQ

Questions, answered

Are you linked to a political party?

No. We don't praise or attack any party or politician. We look at what a rule does for consumers, whoever made it.

Where do your numbers come from?

Official statistics, government releases and published reporting. The source sits next to every figure we use, and we link to it wherever we can.

Why Instagram and not a blog?

Because that's where people are. Our stories, explainers and poll results go out on Instagram first, and the latest posts also appear on our homepage.

What happens to my poll answers?

We read them and share the overall results on Instagram. We never publish anything that identifies you. [link to the privacy notice]

Can journalists use your data?

Yes. Please credit Upside India and the original source. For interviews or data requests, contact our press team.

How can I suggest a topic?

Write to us through the contact page. When lots of people raise the same cost or rule, it goes on our list.

Who funds Upside India?

Upside India is funded by the Consumer Choice Center.
