A day of India's upside

6:00 · The chai stand

Paying for chai takes two seconds

25.47 billionUPI payments in a single month

Scan, pay, done, even at the smallest stall. UPI set a new record in October 2026.

Source: NPCI data, via Entrepreneur India

8:30 · The commute

More ways to get to work

3rdlargest metro network in the world

About 1,100 km of metro across 26 cities. Ride apps now compete for drivers with flat daily fees instead of taking a cut of each fare.

Sources: PIB, March 2026; Outlook Business

12:00 · Phone in hand

Some of the cheapest data on earth

$0.16for 1 GB, against $6.00 in the US

A fraction of what Americans and Europeans pay, so the whole country can stay connected.

Source: Cable.co.uk worldwide mobile data pricing, 2023

16:00 · The shops

A simple, affordable tax on most everyday goods

5% and 18%the two GST rates for most goods

Since 22 September 2025, most everyday goods carry one of just two GST rates, and staples like milk, paneer and bread pay none.

Sources: WION; Business Standard

19:00 · Evening

Lighter drinks, lighter tax

16 → 8excise slabs in Karnataka

Since 11 May 2026, Karnataka taxes drinks by strength: the lighter the drink, the lower the tax.

Source: Dynamite News, 9 October 2026

21:00 · Home

Lower bills at home

50 lakh+homes now run on rooftop solar

Households with rooftop solar report power bills cut by 71% on average.

Sources: pv magazine, August 2026; CEEW survey
